The file is an English onboarding guide for a new employee's first day.

PASS if all of the following hold:
- Steps are a numbered list, and each step is a complete sentence in the active voice that says who does what.
- The guide addresses the reader as "you".
- Paragraphs are short, and headings put the meaningful word first.
- Sentences stay under about 25 words, and there is no formal vocabulary where a short everyday word exists.
- Terms such as repository or task tracker are explained in plain words the first time they appear.

FAIL if the guide is a wall of text, if steps are headline fragments without verbs, if it is written in the passive voice throughout, or if it is padded with formal phrases.
