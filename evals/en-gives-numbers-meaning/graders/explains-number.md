The reply is a short note to a client about a campaign that reached 39% of its annual target in the first quarter.

PASS if the reply gives the figure a meaning a reader grasps at once (for example "about two in five" or "less than half") AND says what that means for the target (for example that one quarter of the year brought more than a quarter of the target, so the campaign is ahead of pace).

FAIL if the reply only repeats "39%" without giving it meaning, or if it states the number without saying what it means for the reader.
